# The Bottleneck Lens — 产业投资人的打法

This is the distinctive module of the skill, distilled from the "industry-investor" method:
**find the chokepoint, not the hype.** Apply it whenever the research is investment-oriented.

---

## The thesis in one line

> 超额收益来自**供给瓶颈**：需求已经存在、而供给结构性稀缺的那个环节，握有定价权。
> Excess return comes from the **supply bottleneck**: the node where demand already exists
> but supply is structurally scarce — that node holds pricing power.

Corollaries:
- **找瓶颈 ≠ 找风口.** The hottest, biggest, most-discussed node ("the model", the headline
  theme) is where crowd sentiment already sits. Edge is found one or more steps away, where
  supply can't easily expand.
- **Timing matters as much as the node.** The asymmetric entry is *before institutional
  money rotates in* (在机构进场之前), then you wait for the rotation (等待机构的轮动) to
  re-rate the node. Buying after the crowd is "涨了哎买" — chasing, not researching.

---

## Procedure (操作步骤)

### A. Trace demand upstream until supply gets scarce (顺产业链溯源)
Start from the visible demand driver and walk **up** the chain, asking at each step
"who supplies *this* node, and can they expand quickly?" Stop where the answer is "no."

> Worked move from the source video: AI boom → **not** the model layer (abundant, crowded)
> → compute → … → **laser chips / optical components** → the deeper upstream, the scarcer
> the supply. The scarce upstream node is the candidate chokepoint.

### B. Score each candidate node (瓶颈打分卡)

Rate 1–5 (5 = strongest bottleneck). A real chokepoint scores high on most rows:

| # | Test | Question | 1 (weak) → 5 (strong) |
|---|------|----------|------------------------|
| 1 | Demand present | Is demand already real and growing, not speculative? | speculative → confirmed & accelerating |
| 2 | Supply constrained | Can supply expand fast (capex lead time, capacity, yield, raw input)? | easily expands → years to add capacity |
| 3 | Concentration | How few players control the node? | fragmented → oligopoly/monopoly |
| 4 | Switching cost / spec-in | Are customers locked in (qualification, certification, integration)? | commodity → designed-in, hard to swap |
| 5 | Pricing power | Has the node actually raised price / expanded margin? | price taker → price maker |
| 6 | Substitution risk | Can a different tech/route bypass this node? | easily bypassed → no near substitute |
| 7 | Crowding / timing | Has institutional money already piled in? | fully discovered → not yet on screens |

**Read the score:** rows 1–6 high + row 7 *low* (undiscovered) = the asymmetric setup. High
1–6 but row 7 also high = a good business but the easy money may be gone — note it honestly.

### C. Locate the timing on the rotation curve (机构轮动)
- **Pre-discovery** — fundamentals visible, sell-side/institutions not yet covering → best
  risk/reward, hardest to source.
- **Early rotation** — coverage initiating, volume rising → thesis confirming.
- **Crowded** — consensus long, valuation rich → returns now depend on momentum/sentiment,
  not the structural edge.
Say plainly which stage the node is in. Do not pretend a crowded node is undiscovered.

### D. Write the bottleneck conclusion
For each chokepoint identified:
- **Node & why it's the bottleneck** (which scorecard rows carry it).
- **Demand driver** pulling through it.
- **Why supply can't catch up** (the mechanism — capex, yield, raw material, IP, regulation).
- **Evidence of pricing power** (price increases, margin expansion, lead times, order books).
- **Timing** (rotation stage) and **what would break the thesis** (capacity coming online,
  substitution, demand stalling).

## Guardrails (重要)

- This module **maps structure; it is not a buy/sell call.** Present the analysis and the
  factors a reader needs to decide for themselves. Add: *this is research, not investment
  advice; not a recommendation to trade.*
- **Falsify it.** Always state the capacity/substitution/demand event that would dissolve the
  bottleneck. A chokepoint with no expiry condition is a story.
- **Beware survivorship & cherry-picking.** A single blogger's headline return (e.g. the
  "+4,502%" in the source video) is anecdote, not proof of method; treat such figures as
  illustration only and never as a track record.
- **Distinguish structural scarcity from temporary shortage.** A shortage that capex will
  fix in 12 months is a trade, not a moat — label which one it is.
