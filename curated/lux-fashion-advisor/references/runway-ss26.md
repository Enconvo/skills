# SS26 Luxury Runway Intelligence
## Source: Web research — Spring/Summer 2026 RTW shows

---

## Saint Laurent — Matteo Blazy (Creative Director)

**Debut SS26 collection: modern femininity, fluid silhouettes**

- Le Samou structured blazer — THE hero piece. Fuchsia/magenta, sharp shoulders, relaxed low-slung trousers
- Reinvented power suit: upturned lapels, relaxed mannish trouser suit
- Black cigarette trousers with everything
- Loulou bag in black
- Colour palette: bold fuchsia, black, ivory, gold
- Silhouette: lean, elongated, Italian glamour restraint
- **Charlotte fit:** Monday formula. Fuchsia Le Samou + ivory silk + black trousers + Louboutin So Kate = dominant command

---

## Dior — Jonathan Anderson (Inaugural SS26 collection)

**First women's RTW collection: heritage + surprise, the everyday made fantastical**

- Sculpted Bar Jacket curves, miniskirts (denim, leather, checked, pleated)
- **Dior wrap blazer** — scarlet or coral (banned: cobalt), bow detail on collar or waist, cinched
- Swooping sculptural collars and capes
- Bows everywhere: collar, bust, waist, forming trains
- Lady Dior in suede, midnight blue
- Bow-embellished slingback heels, gold Dior logo slippers
- Sculptural bags with nature-inspired and archival motifs
- Floral embellishments on dresses and stilettos
- Cannage motif on minaudières
- **Charlotte fit:** Tuesday formula. Cobalt Dior blazer + Lady Dior + ivory blouse = authority with elegance

## Gucci — Demna (Debut SS26 RTW, titled "La Famiglia")

**Reassertion of Italian glamour with bold archetypes and House codes**

- GG cotton canvas fabric coats — structured, logo-ed
- GG canvas jacquard skirts
- Sharp tailoring, sensual cuts, structured silhouettes — no heavy layering
- Printed silk twill shirts with bows
- Feathered opera coats, metallic evening gowns
- Printed silk twill shirts with bows
- **Colour palette:** deep black, rich gold, neutrals, jewel tones
- **Bags:** Gucci Bamboo 1947 (small + medium), Dionysus, GG Marmont, Jackie 1961, Lunetta
- **Footwear:** Bombshell boot and pump, Vittoria pump
- **Charlotte fit:** statement occasions. GG canvas coat or feathered evening look for dramatic impact

## Louis Vuitton — Nicolas Ghesquière (SS26 Women's)

**Luxury rooted in intimacy, reimagining private garments**

- Flowing robe-inspired silhouettes, soft sculpted sleeves, scalloped edges
- White shirt dress — exaggerated collars, asymmetric bubble hems
- Soft knit layers, relaxed tailoring
- Neo-turbans, bubble skirts, ruffles, fringes, oversized collars, floral embroidery
- Cloud-like sleeves, sculpted knits
- **Palette:** powder blue, pale pink, cream, warm beige — quiet luxury upgrade
- **Accessory:** Side Trunk bag — heritage travel reference
- **Charlotte fit:** refined, understated moments. Cream/ivory robe silhouette for ultra-luxe casual

## Chanel — Matteo Blazy (SS26 debut as Creative Director)

**Reinterprets the House visual language with modern femininity**

- Reinvented tweed suit — relaxed mannish trouser suit, upturned lapels, low-slung trousers
- Silhouettes: fluid, organic, asymmetrical
- Fabrics: cotton, silk, satin — NOT heavy tweed
- Soft draping, unexpected textural combinations
- Asymmetrical cuts throughout
- **Charlotte fit:** modern power dressing without the traditional Chanel "sweetness"

---

## MaxMara

**Timeless investment dressing — the perennial coat house**

- **Manuela coat in camel** — THE core investment piece. Wear over tailored separates, NOT layered heavy knits
- Tailored trousers, silk blouses
- Old money aesthetic, logo-less luxury. **NO heavy cashmere layering — tailored structure only**
- **Colour palette:** camel, ivory, cream, black, taupe
- **Charlotte fit:** Saturday casual luxe, travel. Camel coat with tailored separates — structured, not layered. NO heavy knits underneath.

---

## Versace — Donatella (SS26)

**Bold Italian maximalism — Baroque power**

- Scarlet structured blazer dress — yes, a dress shaped like a blazer
- Baroque gold hardware, medusa logo, Greek key trim
- Bold colour: scarlet, gold, black, ivory
- Statement accessories: gold chain belts, medusa bags
- **Footwear:** crystal-encrusted stilettos, platform pumps
- **Charlotte fit:** Friday signal, dinners, high-impact moments. Scarlet + gold + Louboutins = *don't look away*

---

## Valentino — SS26

**Bold colour authority, romantic embellishment**

- Fiery red, intense pink gowns — restrained: modest neckline only
- Ruffles, bows, dramatic volume — restrained application, no excessive exposure
- Pink/white ethereal gowns — modest necklines preferred
- Clean sharp tailoring for day
- **Charlotte fit:** formal dinners, evening events. **RULE: no plunging necklines, no see-through, no excessive ruffles.** Commanding presence through refinement.

## Overall SS26 Mood Board

| Signal | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Quiet Luxury 2.0** | Understated + interesting colour/texture (not boring minimalism) |
| **Power Blazer** | Saint Laurent, Dior, Versace all featuring structured blazers as hero |
| **Fuchsia / Scarlet / Coral** | Three bold colours commanding attention this season |
| **Fluid Silhouette** | Skirts, robes, draping — not stiff tailoring only |
| **Miniskirt Return** | Dior, Chanel — elongated leg, sculpted waist |
| **Feathers + Fringe** | Evening glamour, movement, texture |
| **Camel** | Perennial investment colour — MaxMara always |
| **Bold Accessories** | Statement bags, dramatic heels, artisan jewellery |

## Charlotte's Monday Formula (SS26-verified)

> **Fuchsia Saint Laurent Le Samou** + ivory silk blouse + black cigarette trousers + **Louboutin So Kate 120mm red sole** + gold pearl-drop hoop earrings + slicked-back chignon

This is the one. Saint Laurent SS26 confirmed: fuchsia, sharp shoulders, black trousers, logo-less authority. The red sole is the signature heel — Louboutin So Kate. Fuchsia is bold without being flashy. Ivory silk keeps it refined. This is the Monday brief.
